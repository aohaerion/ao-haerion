# AO Haerion Overview

AO Haerion is an early public project for making complex work easier to understand, decide, organize, and resume.

It focuses on the practical problem of continuity:

> How can a person return to interrupted work without losing the thread, inventing missing context, or confusing decisions with assumptions?

## Core idea

AO Haerion treats complexity as something that must be made readable.

The first public layer is not about automation.
It is about structure, traceability, and restraint.

## Four starting directions

### Clarify a situation

Before acting, the situation should be made understandable.

This means identifying:

- what is known;
- what is uncertain;
- what is missing;
- what needs a decision.

### Make a decision

A decision should remain connected to its context.

A readable decision should show:

- the options considered;
- the chosen direction;
- the reason for the choice;
- the evidence or assumptions behind it.

### Organize a goal

A goal should be translated into a structure that can be followed.

This may include:

- objective;
- constraints;
- next actions;
- risks;
- expected outputs.

### Resume a path

When work is interrupted, resuming should not depend on memory alone.

AO Haerion aims to preserve enough structure to make continuation safer and clearer.

## First public milestone

The first proposed public technical milestone is:

**M001 — Governed Memory Layer**

M001 defines how AO Haerion preserves context, decisions, evidence, and direction across interrupted work.

## What AO Haerion is not claiming now

At this stage, AO Haerion is not claiming to be:

- a complete framework;
- a production system;
- a validated runtime;
- a complete AI agent architecture;
- an automatic truth engine.

## Public development posture

AO Haerion should remain:

- human-readable;
- technically careful;
- traceable;
- limited in public claims;
- open to progressive development.
